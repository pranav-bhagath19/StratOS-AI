# StratOS AI — Design Partner Program

StratOS AI is a bootstrapped, founder-led, pre-seed product with a working MVP. We are
opening a small **design-partner program** to validate — on real targets — whether
recurring, verified competitive intelligence changes how teams decide. This document is
honest about what partners get, what is *not* yet guaranteed, and how data is handled.

**No design partners are claimed as active yet.** This program is in the outreach-preparation
stage.

---

## Who this is for

Teams that research competitors or strategic accounts **manually** today:

- Competitive Intelligence
- Revenue Operations
- Product Marketing
- Strategy
- B2B founders and commercial leaders

Best fit: B2B companies of roughly 50–1,000 employees with a recurring need to track a
defined set of competitors or accounts.

---

## What we will study

For each partner we define one concrete, recurring question — for example:

- "What changed this week across our top 5 competitors?"
- "Is this strategic account showing buying or risk signals?"
- "Did a competitor change pricing, leadership, or product?"

The point is a **repeatable job**, not a one-off lookup — so we can measure whether the
intelligence stays useful over time.

## What analysis we will configure

- Primarily **Account Pulse** (the competitive-intelligence wedge).
- A defined target list (competitor domains or strategic accounts).
- A recurring schedule (e.g., weekly) so briefs refresh and **diff** against the prior run.
- Optional Slack delivery of each brief.

## What the design partner receives

- Recurring Executive Strategic Briefs (score, recommended move, confidence, action pack,
  rationale) on their targets.
- Analysis diffs showing what changed run-over-run.
- Brief export (Markdown / PDF / share link) and optional Slack delivery.
- Direct access to the founder and materially faster response to feedback.
- Influence over the near-term roadmap.

## What feedback we want

- **Decision usefulness** — did a brief change or inform a decision?
- **Freshness** — was the signal recent enough to matter?
- **Source coverage** — did it reach the sources you care about?
- **Trust** — did the evidence/inference/confidence separation earn your trust?
- **Recurring value** — would you keep it running?

## What is *not* yet guaranteed

We are pre-seed and say so plainly:

- No SLA, uptime, or support guarantees during the pilot.
- No accuracy, performance, cost-saving, or time-saving guarantees — briefs are **decision
  support, not ground truth**, and must be independently validated before high-stakes action.
- The cost-minimizing adaptive router is designed but not yet shipped.
- Live behavior depends on our provider API credentials being configured.
- Features may change based on what we learn.

## How data is handled

- StratOS accesses **public** web data only; it does not attempt to access private or
  authenticated content.
- We will agree on the target list with each partner; we do not need access to partner
  internal systems to run analyses.
- Secrets live only in server-side, git-ignored configuration.
- We will delete a partner's analysis data on request.
- Anything a partner shares in confidence stays confidential; no partner is named publicly
  without written peranalysis.

## Metrics we will evaluate

Framework only — these have **no values yet**; producing them is the goal of the program:

- Repeated-analysis / scheduled-run usage.
- Partner-rated decision usefulness.
- Freshness at delivery.
- Source coverage per analysis.
- Qualitative "would you keep it running" signal.
